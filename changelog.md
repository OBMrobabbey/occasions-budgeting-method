## [2026‑05‑20] Structural Update: Flexible Pre‑Declaration Range and Revised Logs

### Summary
Refined the core structure of The Celebrations Budget (OBM Framework) to introduce a flexible range for start‑of‑year pre‑declarations and to clarify the behavioural roles of the three core documents. This update strengthens the method’s intentionality, preserves scarcity, and improves year‑to‑year adaptability.

### Changes
- **Pre‑Declared Occasions Register**
  - Updated from a fixed 14‑slot model to a flexible **12–16 slot** range.
  - Reinforced the principle that this register is completed once at the start of the year and remains immutable.
  - Clarified that unused slots remain blank and are not carried into the Declaration Log.

- **Occasions Declaration Log**
  - Expanded to support **up to 8 Declared‑Later Occasions**, depending on how many were pre‑declared.
  - Added a dedicated section for the **2 Floating Occasions**, which remain fixed and spontaneous.
  - Total dynamic capacity: **10 entries** (8 declared‑later + 2 floating).

- **Occasions Reflection Log**
  - Confirmed as a full **22‑slot** log capturing all Occasions:
    - Pre‑Declared
    - Declared‑Later
    - Floating
  - Added a category field to classify each Occasion type.

### Rationale
- Introduces **flexibility** without weakening the behavioural mechanics.
- Preserves the distinction between:
  - **Start‑of‑year commitments** (intentional, scarce, fixed)
  - **Mid‑year decisions** (deliberate, accountable)
  - **Spontaneous moments** (strictly limited to two)
- Ensures the system remains robust across years with varying levels of predictability.
- Aligns all templates with the 22‑Occasion annual allocation.

### Files Updated

- `templates/occasions-reflection-log.md`

### Notes
This update formalises the flexible‑range model as the canonical structure for OBM going forward.
