# Structure of the Annual Allocation
Part of The Celebrations Budget (OBM Framework)

The Celebrations Budget is built around a fixed annual allocation of **22 Occasions**.  
These are divided into three behavioural categories that balance intentionality, flexibility, and spontaneity.

This document describes the operational structure of the method and how the annual allocation is organised across the year.

---

## 1. Pre‑Declared Occasions (12–16)

At the start of each year, between **12 and 16 Occasions** are formally pre‑declared.  
These represent meaningful events already known in advance—holidays, birthdays, anniversaries, and other significant commitments.

Characteristics:

- Completed once at the start of the year  
- Recorded in the **Pre‑Declared Occasions Register**  
- The register is **fixed and not edited** after January  
- Any unused slots remain blank  
- These Occasions form the intentional foundation of the year

This flexible range allows for years with more predictability (closer to 16) and years with less (closer to 12).

---

## 2. Declared‑Later Occasions (up to 8)

During the year, additional Occasions may be declared **in advance** of the event.  
The number available depends on how many were pre‑declared:

- If 16 were pre‑declared → up to 4 declared‑later  
- If 12 were pre‑declared → up to 8 declared‑later  

These Occasions are recorded in the **Occasions Declaration Log** at the moment they are declared.

Characteristics:

- Declared before the Occasion begins  
- Represent meaningful events that emerge as the year unfolds  
- Provide flexibility without undermining the intentional structure  
- Maximum capacity: **8**

---

## 3. Floating Occasions (2)

Two Occasions each year are reserved for **true spontaneity**.  
These are declared **on the day itself**, without advance planning.

Characteristics:

- Always exactly **2**  
- Logged in a dedicated section of the Declaration Log  
- Preserve space for unanticipated meaning  
- Reinforce the behavioural boundary between planning and spontaneity

---

## 4. Total Annual Allocation (22)

Regardless of how the year unfolds, the total number of Occasions remains fixed at **22**:

- **12–16 Pre‑Declared**  
- **Up to 8 Declared‑Later**  
- **2 Floating**

This fixed total creates a behavioural boundary that encourages deliberation, reflection, and intentional use of time.

---

## 5. Reflection Across All Occasions

All 22 Occasions—regardless of category—are reviewed in the **Occasions Reflection Log** at the end of the year.

The reflection process supports:

- meaning‑making  
- behavioural calibration  
- long‑term refinement of the method  
- understanding which Occasions were worth declaring  

The Reflection Log is the final record of the year.

---

## 6. Document Interaction Summary

- **Pre‑Declared Register**  
  - Completed once at the start of the year  
  - Contains 12–16 Occasions  
  - Immutable

- **Declaration Log**  
  - Used throughout the year  
  - Contains up to 8 declared‑later Occasions  
  - Contains 2 floating Occasions  
  - Dynamic

- **Reflection Log**  
  - Completed after each Occasion  
  - Contains all 22 Occasions  
  - Integrates both planned and emergent meaning

Together, these documents form the operational backbone of The Celebrations Budget.
