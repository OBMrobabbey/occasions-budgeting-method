# Occasions Declaration Log
Part of The Celebrations Budget (OBM Framework)

This log records Occasions declared **during the year**, including:

- **Declared‑Later Occasions** (typically 4–8, depending on how many were pre‑declared)
- **Floating Occasions** (always 2), declared on the day

These entries are made *before* the Occasion begins, except for the two floating Occasions.
