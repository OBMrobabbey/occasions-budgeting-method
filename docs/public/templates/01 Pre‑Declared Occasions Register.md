# Pre‑Declared Occasions Register
Part of The Celebrations Budget (OBM Framework)

This register is completed at the start of the year.  
You may pre‑declare **between 12 and 16 Occasions**, depending on how much of your year is known in advance.

Once completed, this register is **fixed and not edited** during the year.
